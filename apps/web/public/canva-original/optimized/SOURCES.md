# Responsive editorial photography

These are resized, JPEG-reencoded versions of the pre-existing Canva export photographs in `../images/`, not AI-generated images or new Maison Sillage product photographs.

For each ID below, `<id>-640.jpg`, `<id>-1280.jpg`, and `<id>-1920.jpg` derive from `../images/<id>.jpeg`, preserving aspect ratio, with JPEG quality 82:

4456729, 11065063, 9563075, 33401683, 7716936, 40169081, 33034129, 11411290, 4936952, 29554115, 4938328.

The original export and its filenames are retained. Sources for the separate classic menswear references are in `../../images/SOURCES.md`.

The eleven 1280px variants total 3,053,518 bytes, compared with 33,923,061 bytes for their originals (about 91% less). This is a file-size comparison, not a measured real-user page-speed improvement. Browsers choose a variant through `srcset` and `sizes`.

The locally hosted Inter variable font comes from https://github.com/rsms/inter/blob/master/docs/font-files/InterVariable.woff2 and is covered by `../INTER-LICENSE.txt`. Existing Cormorant Garamond files remain unchanged.
